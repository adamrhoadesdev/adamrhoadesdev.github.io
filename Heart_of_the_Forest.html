Heart of the Forest

A 3D tower-defense game with third-person building and combat.

EngineTeam Wombat Custom OpenGL Engine
TeamSeven-person student team
DateJanuary–April 2022

Building the Game

Our team used our custom-built OpenGL engine to create a 3D tower-defense game inspired by Orcs Must Die and Dungeon Defenders. The player could construct defenses while also moving through the level and participating directly in combat.

Team and Iteration

I guided teamwork through scrums, co-led design meetings, and supported branching and pull-request workflows. I also collaborated on a map and level editor that accelerated content creation. Throughout development, we reviewed player feedback covering controls, camera behavior, construction, combat, responsiveness, movement, model clarity, learning, UI/UX, and mechanics, then used those findings to improve the game.
