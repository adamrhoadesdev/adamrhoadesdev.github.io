Quoobe Town: Vibrant Dreams

A 24-hour Unity game about painting a city to advance through levels.

EngineUnity
TeamRapid Solo Project
Date24-Hour Project

Rapid Game Development

I created Quoobe Town: Vibrant Dreams in Unity during a 24-hour development period. The central mechanic asked the player to paint the city in order to progress through each level.

Enemy Roles

Enemy AI supported the core mechanic in two distinct ways. Some enemies pursued the player directly, while others cleaned up the paint left behind, forcing the player to balance movement, coverage, and pressure while advancing.
